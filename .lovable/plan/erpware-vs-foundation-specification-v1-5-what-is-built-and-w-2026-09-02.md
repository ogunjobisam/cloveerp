# Clove ERP vs Foundation Specification v1.5 — what is built and what is not

Read-only audit. The spec grew from v1 (499 lines) to v1.2 (23 parts, 34 decisions) and then v1.5 (40 pages, 22 parts, 38 decisions; v1.2 had no Part 13, so every part from device operations onward is numbered one lower than it was, and v1.3 and v1.4 are skipped on the author's instruction). This maps every part against the migrations, the public `erp_*` doors, and the route/UI tree as they exist today. Version bumps since v1.2 add §17.5–§17.11 (the platform's own commercial process) and D34–D38.

## Headline summary

**Built and working:** the entire foundation (B1–B11), all six Addendum B configuration surfaces, the content-pack system, tenant lifecycle including cryptographic deletion, the platform-owner control plane, and every operational module with screens — roughly the spec's Parts 1–13.

**The four builds v1.2 added, all now closed:**
1. **Part 13 — Device operations**: database surface Sep 4, the scan-first client at `/device` Sep 5.
2. **Part 14 — Output subsystem**: common model Sep 4; routing, digest, escalation, quiet hours, delivery tracking, print routing and queue health, sender identity and ZPL Sep 5.
3. **Part 17 — Commercial & entitlement**: built Sep 4, meters wired and asserted recorded.
4. **Part 19 depth — migration reconciliation & cutover**: built Sep 4.

**The one build v1.5 added, closed Sep 6:** §17.5–§17.11, the platform running its own commercial process on its own primitives — price book, quotes with live margin, contracts that provision, renewals, invoices, revenue, and the customer's own agreement — in four migrations (`20260904590000` to `20260904620000`) with four suites (18, 22, 19 and 21 cases), plus D34's refusal register (`20260904630000`).

**Closed Sep 5:** Part 16's scoped communication, maintenance announcements and disclosure timelines; Part 18's extracts, subscriptions, packs and analytics contract; §7.18's interview screen was already built. Part 20, Part 21 and §9.4 were closed Sep 4.

**What remains outside the database by decision:** the public status page (§16.4), voice picking (§13.1), EPL and IPL composition (§14.3), delivery transmission (the gateway), natural-language querying (a client of the analytics contract), and promotion of notification and print routes (recorded open).

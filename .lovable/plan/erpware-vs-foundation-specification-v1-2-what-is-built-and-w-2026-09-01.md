# Clove ERP vs Foundation Specification v1.2 — what is built and what is not

Read-only audit. The spec grew from v1 (499 lines) to v1.2 (2,808 lines, 23 parts, 34 decisions). This maps every part against the 145 migrations, ~319 public `erp_*` functions, and the route/UI tree as they exist today.

## Headline summary

**Built and working:** the entire foundation (B1–B11), all six Addendum B configuration surfaces, the content-pack system, tenant lifecycle including cryptographic deletion, the platform-owner control plane, and every operational module with screens — roughly the spec's Parts 1–13.

**The four builds v1.2 added, all now closed:**
1. **Part 14 — Device operations**: database surface Sep 4, the scan-first client at `/device` Sep 5.
2. **Part 15 — Output subsystem**: common model Sep 4; routing, digest, escalation, quiet hours, delivery tracking, print routing and queue health, sender identity and ZPL Sep 5.
3. **Part 18 — Commercial & entitlement**: built Sep 4, meters wired and asserted recorded.
4. **Part 20 depth — migration reconciliation & cutover**: built Sep 4.

**Closed Sep 5:** Part 17's scoped communication, maintenance announcements and disclosure timelines; Part 19's extracts, subscriptions, packs and analytics contract; §7.18's interview screen was already built. Part 21, Part 22 and §9.4 were closed Sep 4.

**What remains outside the database by decision:** the public status page (§17.4), voice picking (§14.1), EPL and IPL composition (§15.3), delivery transmission (the gateway), natural-language querying (a client of the analytics contract), and promotion of notification and print routes (recorded open).
